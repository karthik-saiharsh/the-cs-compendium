Welcome to the CS Compendium.

I am Karthik. This is a personal chronicle, written in the hope that it can serve both my own needs and the needs of others who walk the same path.

## Why this exists

Every text has an origin, and this one began with a simple realization truth.

When I stepped out as a computer science graduate and began looking for work, I ran into an unexpected problem: too much information that is not structured well enough. There are more resources now than ever, but quantity is not the same as usefulness.

When an interview is only a week away, nobody has the luxury of reading a thousand page text book on Object Oriented Programming or Algorithms. What one needs is a battle manual. Concise, direct, and focused on the essentials.

## What I found

The internet offered little comfort. On one side were long, bloated textbooks; on the other were scattered articles on each topic written by various authors each with a different style of explanation.

There were large archives like GeeksforGeeks, certainly. They are valuable in their own right, but they were not written in the format I wanted when I was trying to sharpen my understanding quickly.

After a month of searching, I reached a blunt conclusion: the tool I needed did not exist.

## What I built

If you want a sturdy road, sometimes you have to pave it yourself. So I started drafting my own revision handbooks.

It did not take long to realize that if I was feeling this gap, others probably were too. The CS Compendium was born from that shared need.

## A note to the reader

This archive is not meant as a shortcut for beginners. It is for practitioners, people who already know the craft and need a swift warm up before stepping into the arena. 

Bear in mind that this is a personal project, assembled largely by myself and whatever friends I could persuade to help

I shall endeavour, to the very best of my ability, to keep the contents of this page accurate and thoroughly fact-checked. Yet I am only human, and therefore prone to all the usual failings of the breed. Should you discover that I have fallen into error, I invite you to take up the mantle of correcting my ignorance and submit a pull request on GitHub containing the amended source.


**I hope it serves you well.**
