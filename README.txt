# The CS Compendium

This is my attempt at compiling all of my knowledge of Computer Science in one place.
Though it is a singular effort on my part, any contributions from anyone on any topic are welcome.

There is no particular schedule or pattern of updates.
I will keep adding new sections and topics as I find the time.

Please note that I do not claim the content to be entirely original. This project is essentially my personal notebook, where I document what I learn and share it with everyone.
I will cite my sources whenever I write about something that has been explained by someone else.

## AI Use
Please note that compiling such a huge material alone is a demanding task.
Hence, I use AI to generate the write-ups when I am short on time. 

However, please do note that the handbook is not simply AI generated.
When I wish to include a topic in this handbook, I first write a short write-up of my own, by hand, one that encapsulates the main idea I wish to present and the way in which I wish to present it.
Then I hand over my succinct write-up to an AI, and have it craft a more detailed version of my content. I then review the generated text and only include it here if I am satisfied with it.
You can therefore be rest assured that this site is not merely AI Slop.
